## Platform Compatibility

### SDL1 Backend is Limited to GNU/Linux

Note that SDL1 is at the very edge of our
compatibility support. 

Our support for SDL1 is limited to GNU/Linux with
GCC or Clang compilers.

We only support SDL1 for accessibility on some older
GNU/Linux distributions. I personally test on CentOS 7
that does not offer SDL2 in their official repository.

So, to keep things simple, and not require building
SDL2 from source or getting it from a third party
repository, we included support for SDL1 builds on
GNU/Linux platforms with modern enough GCC and Clang
compilers.

So, although SDL1 support somehow exist; It is strongly
suggested to build the project with SDL2 or SDL3.

Extra tiny note: If you are thinking about taking the
advantage of the current SDL1 support to extend it and
port the project to other older platforms, you are
welcome to do as your inner hacker desire.
